1/3 Weighting based on useful life [A]
    Formula: (* remaining-life remaining-life)
    Working: 10 × 10 = 100
1/3 Weighting based on useful life [B]
    Formula: (* remaining-life remaining-life)
    Working: 20 × 20 = 400
1/3 Weighting based on useful life [C]
    Formula: (* remaining-life remaining-life)
    Working: 20 × 20 = 400
1/4 Carrying amount after weighting [A]
    Formula: (* carrying-amount weighting)
    Working: 100 × 100 = 10,000
1/4 Carrying amount after weighting [B]
    Formula: (* carrying-amount weighting)
    Working: 150 × 400 = 60,000
1/4 Carrying amount after weighting [C]
    Formula: (* carrying-amount weighting)
    Working: 200 × 400 = 80,000
1/5 Pro-rata allocation factor [A]
    Formula: (decimal/divide weighted-amount (dim/sum all.weighted-amount) 6)
    Working: decimal/divide(10,000; dim/sum({:A 10,000; :B 60,000; :C 80,000}); 6) = 6.7 %
1/5 Pro-rata allocation factor [B]
    Formula: (decimal/divide weighted-amount (dim/sum all.weighted-amount) 6)
    Working: decimal/divide(60,000; dim/sum({:A 10,000; :B 60,000; :C 80,000}); 6) = 40.0 %
1/5 Pro-rata allocation factor [C]
    Formula: (decimal/divide weighted-amount (dim/sum all.weighted-amount) 6)
    Working: decimal/divide(80,000; dim/sum({:A 10,000; :B 60,000; :C 80,000}); 6) = 53.3 %
1/6 Allocation of the headquarters building [A]
    Formula: (alloc/pro-rata allocable-corporate all.weighted-amount rounding)
    Working: alloc/pro-rata(150; {:A 10,000; :B 60,000; :C 80,000}; 0) = 10
1/6 Allocation of the headquarters building [B]
    Formula: (alloc/pro-rata allocable-corporate all.weighted-amount rounding)
    Working: alloc/pro-rata(150; {:A 10,000; :B 60,000; :C 80,000}; 0) = 60
1/6 Allocation of the headquarters building [C]
    Formula: (alloc/pro-rata allocable-corporate all.weighted-amount rounding)
    Working: alloc/pro-rata(150; {:A 10,000; :B 60,000; :C 80,000}; 0) = 80
1/7 Carrying amount after allocation [A]
    Formula: carrying-amount + corporate-share
    Working: 100 + 10 = 110
1/7 Carrying amount after allocation [B]
    Formula: carrying-amount + corporate-share
    Working: 150 + 60 = 210
1/7 Carrying amount after allocation [C]
    Formula: carrying-amount + corporate-share
    Working: 200 + 80 = 280
2/2 Recoverable amount (higher of value in use and FVLCD) [A]
    Formula: max(cgu.value-in-use; cgu.fvlcd)
    Working: max(Value in use 199; Fair value less costs of disposal n/a) → Value in use = 199
2/2 Recoverable amount (higher of value in use and FVLCD) [B]
    Formula: max(cgu.value-in-use; cgu.fvlcd)
    Working: max(Value in use 164; Fair value less costs of disposal n/a) → Value in use = 164
2/2 Recoverable amount (higher of value in use and FVLCD) [C]
    Formula: max(cgu.value-in-use; cgu.fvlcd)
    Working: max(Value in use 271; Fair value less costs of disposal n/a) → Value in use = 271
2/3 Impairment loss [A]
    Formula: (max 0 (- tested-amount recoverable-amount))
    Working: max(0; 110 − 199) = –
2/3 Impairment loss [B]
    Formula: (max 0 (- tested-amount recoverable-amount))
    Working: max(0; 210 − 164) = 46
2/3 Impairment loss [C]
    Formula: (max 0 (- tested-amount recoverable-amount))
    Working: max(0; 280 − 271) = 9
3/1 To goodwill [A]
    Formula: (min impairment-loss (or cgu.goodwill 0))
    Working: min(0; or(nil; 0)) = –
3/1 To goodwill [B]
    Formula: (min impairment-loss (or cgu.goodwill 0))
    Working: min(46; or(nil; 0)) = –
3/1 To goodwill [C]
    Formula: (min impairment-loss (or cgu.goodwill 0))
    Working: min(9; or(nil; 0)) = –
3/2 To the allocated headquarters building [A]
    Formula: (get (alloc/pro-rata (- impairment-loss loss-to-goodwill) {:corporate corporate-share :own (- carrying-amount (or cgu.goodwill 0))} rounding) :corporate)
    Working: get(alloc/pro-rata(0 − 0; {:corporate 10; :own 100 − or(nil; 0)}; 0); :corporate) = –
3/2 To the allocated headquarters building [B]
    Formula: (get (alloc/pro-rata (- impairment-loss loss-to-goodwill) {:corporate corporate-share :own (- carrying-amount (or cgu.goodwill 0))} rounding) :corporate)
    Working: get(alloc/pro-rata(46 − 0; {:corporate 60; :own 150 − or(nil; 0)}; 0); :corporate) = 13
3/2 To the allocated headquarters building [C]
    Formula: (get (alloc/pro-rata (- impairment-loss loss-to-goodwill) {:corporate corporate-share :own (- carrying-amount (or cgu.goodwill 0))} rounding) :corporate)
    Working: get(alloc/pro-rata(9 − 0; {:corporate 80; :own 200 − or(nil; 0)}; 0); :corporate) = 3
3/3 To the other assets of the CGU [A]
    Formula: (- impairment-loss loss-to-goodwill loss-to-corporate)
    Working: 0 − 0 − 0 = –

    Working: 46 − 0 − 13 = 33

3/4 Impairment loss allocated [C]

    Working: 0 + 3 + 6 = 9
4/1 Carrying amount of the CGUs (A, B, C)
    Formula: (dim/sum carrying-amount)
    Working: dim/sum({:A 100; :B 150; :C 200}) = 450

    Working: 450 + 150 + 50 = 650
4/5 Impairment losses recognised in steps 2–3
    Formula: (dim/sum loss-allocated)
    Working: dim/sum({:A 0; :B 46; :C 9}) = 55
4/6 Carrying amount of the group after step 1
    Formula: group-before − group-step1-losses
    Working: 650 − 55 = 595
4/8 Impairment loss of the group
    Formula: (max 0 (- group-carrying-amount group-ra))
    Working: max(0; 595 − 720) = –
5/1 Goodwill
    Formula: (dim/sum loss-to-goodwill)
    Working: dim/sum({:A 0; :B 0; :C 0}) = –
5/2 Other assets of the CGUs
    Formula: (dim/sum loss-to-own-assets)
    Working: dim/sum({:A 0; :B 33; :C 6}) = 39
5/3 Headquarters building
    Formula: (dim/sum loss-to-corporate)
    Working: dim/sum({:A 0; :B 13; :C 3}) = 16
5/5 Total impairment loss

    Working: 0 + 39 + 16 + 0 = 55
